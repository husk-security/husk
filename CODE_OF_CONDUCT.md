# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the
husk community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Security research

husk is a security tool, and security research is welcome. Reporting a
vulnerability, a false negative, or a way to defeat a detection is never
unwelcome behavior. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for anything affecting
husk itself, and do not test against systems you are not authorized to test.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior to
**erik.frankling@frankling.se**. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly. Project maintainers will respect the privacy
and security of the reporter of any incident.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will
communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate. Repeated or
severe violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from any sort of
interaction with the project.
